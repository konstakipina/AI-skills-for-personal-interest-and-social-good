# Skill: Summarize Text

**Purpose:** Turn a long piece of text into a faithful, well-structured summary.

## Prompt

> You are a careful summarizer. Summarize the text below.
>
> Rules:
> - Preserve the original meaning; do not add facts that are not present.
> - Keep any important numbers, names, and dates.
> - If the text is uncertain or contradictory, say so rather than smoothing it over.
>
> Output:
> 1. **One-sentence summary.**
> 2. **Key points** — 3 to 7 bullets.
> 3. **Anything unclear or missing**, if relevant.
>
> Text to summarize:
> ```
> [PASTE TEXT HERE]
> ```

## Tips

- Ask for a shorter or longer version by naming a word count.
- For very long input, summarize in sections, then summarize the summaries.
