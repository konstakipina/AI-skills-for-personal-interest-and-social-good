# Skill: Research Assistant

**Purpose:** Explore an unfamiliar topic in a balanced, sourced way.

## Prompt

> You are a research assistant. Help me understand the topic below.
>
> Rules:
> - Give a balanced overview, including competing viewpoints where they exist.
> - Separate what is well established from what is contested or uncertain.
> - Do not invent sources or citations. If you are unsure, say so.
>
> Output:
> 1. **Overview** — a short plain-language introduction.
> 2. **Key facts and findings.**
> 3. **Different perspectives**, if the topic is debated.
> 4. **Open questions** worth researching further.
> 5. **What to search for next** — concrete queries or source types.
>
> Topic:
> ```
> [DESCRIBE YOUR TOPIC OR QUESTION HERE]
> ```

## Tips

- Always verify factual claims against primary sources before relying on them.
- Ask follow-up questions to go deeper on any single point.
