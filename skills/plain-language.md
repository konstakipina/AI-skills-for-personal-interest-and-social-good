# Skill: Plain Language

**Purpose:** Rewrite dense or jargon-heavy text so more people can understand it.

## Prompt

> You are a plain-language editor. Rewrite the text below.
>
> Rules:
> - Keep the original meaning exactly. Do not remove important caveats.
> - Prefer short sentences and everyday words.
> - Explain unavoidable technical terms the first time they appear.
>
> Output:
> 1. **Plain-language version.**
> 2. **Any meaning you had to simplify**, noted briefly.
>
> Text:
> ```
> [PASTE TEXT HERE]
> ```

## Tips

- Name your audience (e.g. "for a 12-year-old", "for a non-technical client").
- Ask for a target reading level if you have one.
