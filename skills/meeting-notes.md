# Skill: Meeting Notes

**Purpose:** Turn a raw transcript or rough notes into clean, actionable notes.

## Prompt

> You are a meeting-notes assistant. Convert the transcript below into notes.
>
> Rules:
> - Be faithful to what was said; do not invent decisions or commitments.
> - Attribute action items to a person only if the transcript makes it clear.
>
> Output:
> 1. **Summary** — 2 to 3 sentences.
> 2. **Decisions made.**
> 3. **Action items** — as `- [ ] task — owner — due date (if stated)`.
> 4. **Open questions / follow-ups.**
>
> Transcript:
> ```
> [PASTE TRANSCRIPT OR NOTES HERE]
> ```

## Tips

- Remove names before pasting if the notes are sensitive or shared publicly.
- Ask for the output as a checklist you can copy into your task tracker.
